    • About this product

    Transform your workspace with the stylish comfort of the Staples Dexley ergonomic mesh task chair.

    Upgrade your workspace with the Staples Dexley Mesh Task Chair, designed for optimal comfort and functionality. Tailored to your body, its adjustable seat height and armrests ensure personalized support, while the adjustable lumbar support promotes proper posture. The synchro-tilt mechanism, upright tilt lock, and tension knob offer control over reclining resistance for focused productivity. With a stable five-star base and smooth casters, maneuvering around the office is effortless. Plus, its breathable mesh construction keeps you cool during long hours. Experience superior ergonomics with features like adjustable headrest, lumbar support, tilt tension, and tilt lock, enhancing your workspace comfort and efficiency. Get one of the best seats in the house for discerning professionals who demand excellence they can count on for years to come.
